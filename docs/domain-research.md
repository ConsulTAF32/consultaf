## A. The Romanian SME landscape (why this market exists)

- **~816,000 SMEs** in Romania (2023); **~772,000 are micro-enterprises** (≤9 employees) — the vast majority. — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/880036/number-of-smes-in-romania/)
- **99.7%** of all Romanian companies are SMEs. — [European Parliament Q E-000602/2024](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/E-9-2024-000602_EN.html)
- Romania has **29 SMEs per 1,000 inhabitants vs. an EU average of 58** — under-developed SME density. — [European Parliament](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/E-9-2024-000602_EN.html)
- **SME productivity is ~40% lower** than large companies in Romania. — [European Parliament](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/E-9-2024-000602_EN.html)

**Takeaway for ConsulTAF:** the target market is enormous (hundreds of thousands of micro/small firms) and structurally under-organized + under-productive — exactly the gap operational consulting closes.

## B. The digitalization gap (the "digitalizare" pillar)

- Only **51% of Romanian SMEs have a website** (EU average **78%**). — [EIB, Digitalisation of SMEs in Romania](https://www.eib.org/en/publications/20230198-digitalisation-of-smes-in-romania)
- Only **17% use ERP or CRM** systems (EU averages 38% / 32%). — [EIB](https://www.eib.org/en/publications/20230198-digitalisation-of-smes-in-romania)
- Romanian SMEs score **lowest in the EU on basic digital intensity**. — [Romania 2025 Digital Decade Country Report](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/romania-2025-digital-decade-country-report)
- Main barriers: **limited digital skills, knowledge, and access to finance.** — [EIB](https://www.eib.org/en/publications/20230198-digitalisation-of-smes-in-romania)

**Takeaway:** "digitalizare prin instrumente simple" (Google Workspace, evidențe, control) is not a luxury — most RO small firms haven't done the basics yet.

## C. Compliance pressure (the "controale / ANAF / SPV" pillar) — RO-specific, fast-changing

- **RO e-Factura:** mandatory **B2B since 1 Jan 2024**; extended to **B2C from 1 Jan 2025**. — [vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/romania/romania-b2b-ro-e-invoicing-efactura-update/)
- Invoices must be **submitted within 5 calendar days** of issuance and **archived 10 years**. — [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/03/23/romania-e-invoicing-2024-key-deadlines-anaf-requirements-and-simplified-ro-e-factura-connectivity/)
- **Penalty of 15% of the invoice value** for failing to issue via RO e-Factura (from July 2024); fines **RON 1,000–2,500** for late/missing submissions. — [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/03/23/romania-e-invoicing-2024-key-deadlines-anaf-requirements-and-simplified-ro-e-factura-connectivity/)
- **SAF-T (D406):** large taxpayers since Jan 2022, **medium since Jan 2023, small taxpayers from Jan 2025.** — [Sovos](https://sovos.com/en-gb/vat/tax-rules/romania-saf-t/)
- **RO e-Transport:** enforcement/penalties from **1 Jan 2025.** — [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/12/02/briefing-document-romanian-e-invoicing-and-e-transport-regulations/)

**Takeaway:** small firms are now inside the same digital-reporting net as big ones — but without the back-office. Being "pregătit pentru controale" has real, dated stakes.

## D. Why businesses struggle (universal evidence — the "pain" hook)

- **~20% of small businesses fail in year 1; ~50% within 5 years.** — [Crestmont Capital](https://www.crestmontcapital.com/blog/small-business-failure-rate-statistics-2026)
- **Cash-flow problems are present in 82% of small-business failures** (SCORE/U.S. Bank). Important nuance: not the root cause in all 82%, but the common trigger. — [SCORE](https://www.score.org/resource/blog-post/1-reason-small-businesses-fail-and-how-avoid-it)
- **Running out of cash drives ~29%** of failures; **poor management ~19%.** — [altLINE](https://altline.sobanco.com/why-do-small-businesses-fail/)

**Takeaway:** a firm can look like it "merge" and still be one cash crunch from trouble. Visibility + control is survival, not bureaucracy.

## E. The cost of disorganization & poor stock control (the "stocuri / costuri" pillars)

- **Inventory distortion cost retailers ~$1.77 trillion in 2023 — 7.2% of all retail sales.** — [ToolsGroup](https://www.toolsgroup.com/blog/the-hidden-costs-of-poor-inventory-management-how-much-are-you-really-losing/)
- **Stock-outs cause ~40% of lost potential sales** industry-wide. — [ToolsGroup](https://www.toolsgroup.com/blog/the-hidden-costs-of-poor-inventory-management-how-much-are-you-really-losing/)
- Holding **excess inventory costs 25–32% of its value per year** (carrying cost). — [ToolsGroup](https://www.toolsgroup.com/blog/the-hidden-costs-of-poor-inventory-management-how-much-are-you-really-losing/)
- Best-in-class operations lose just **2.1% of sales to stockouts**; struggling ones lose **11–16%.** — [Anchor Group](https://www.anchorgroup.tech/blog/wholesale-inventory-management-statistics)

**Takeaway:** "stocuri neclare" isn't a tidiness issue — it's a double money leak (lost sales + carrying cost) measured in double-digit percentages.

## F. Quotes — verified attributions only

> ⚠️ **Do NOT use** "What gets measured gets managed" attributed to Drucker — he never said it; it derives from V.F. Ridgway's 1956 paper *Dysfunctional Consequences of Performance Measurements*, which actually warned *against* careless measurement. — [Centre for Public Impact](https://medium.com/centre-for-public-impact/what-gets-measured-gets-managed-its-wrong-and-drucker-never-said-it-fe95886d3df6)

Safe to use (well-documented):
- **"Efficiency is doing things right; effectiveness is doing the right things."** — Peter Drucker
- **"The greatest danger in times of turbulence is not the turbulence — it is to act with yesterday's logic."** — Peter Drucker
- **"Without data, you're just another person with an opinion."** — widely attributed to W. Edwards Deming

(For Romanian copy, paraphrase as proverbs where exact attribution is uncertain, or attribute clearly.)
